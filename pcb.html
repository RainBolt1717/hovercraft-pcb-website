About the PCB

This PCB was designed for a DIY RC hovercraft race hosted by Zen Maker Lab.
It was a collaborative effort between Connor Mcquade and myself, Nyrin Woelfle.
The board supports key electronics used in the hovercraft, enabling fast, lightweight, and agile performance.
